• Your vote is your attendance: “coming” seats you for the night, “not coming” excuses you without a court penalty. Only an unannounced no-show drops a court.
•
• Spares: when a regular votes “not coming”, a seat opens and spares who said “available” reserve seats in answer order; payment verification confirms them; the rest stay on standby.
• Verified no-show: one court down next week. A missing vote alone is not a no-show.
• Courts hold four. When more than 24 play (all 26 regulars, or a spare joins), the extra players become a fifth player on the bottom courts — Court 6 first, then Court 5, then Court 4. With fewer than 24, nobody plays alone: a single leftover player joins the last court in use as its fifth player, and a court left with two or three plays singles. A court of five plays five doubles games to 15 points and each player sits out one; a court of four plays three games to 21.
• Games end at the target: first to 21 (15 on a court of five) wins, even at 20–20 — no deuce.
• Arrive 5 minutes early. Players arriving more than 5 minutes late move down one court. On the bottom court in use there is no lower court, so a late player stays there.
• Shuttlecocks: two per player on each court — 8 for four players, 10 for five, 6 for three and 4 for two. Players provide any extra shuttlecocks.
• Every session is two ladder rounds. After the second round the courts are free for casual games or drills (use old shuttlecocks).
• The admin sets the initial seeding. After each round the player with the most wins on a court moves up one court and the fewest moves down; ties go to points, then point difference, then a coin toss that the app does by itself (the same result on every phone). Nobody moves down from the last court in use.
• The app can make mistakes. Please keep a note of your court's score for each round on your phone. If you notice an error, send your recorded scores to the organizer on the same day, after the session.

League Format & Rules
Please read all rules carefully before agreeing.
  1. Arrive 5 minutes early. Players arriving more than 5 minutes late move down one court. On the bottom court in use there is no lower court, so a late player stays there.
  2. Warm-up is limited to 5 minutes at the start and must be done only on your assigned court. Do not use new shuttlecocks for warm-up — use old/used ones only. Once games begin, no more warm-ups are allowed.
  3. Each court gets two shuttlecocks per player: 8 for a court of four, 10 for a court of five (6 for three, 4 for two). Players provide any extra shuttlecocks.
  4. Games are played to 21 points — 15 on a court of five. First to reach the target wins, even at 20-20 (14-14 on a court of five). No deuce.
  5. Scores are entered by one person per court after confirming with all players. The app shows exactly who plays who in each game (Game 1: A+B vs C+D · Game 2: A+C vs B+D · Game 3: A+D vs B+C).
  6. The app can make mistakes. Please keep a note of your court's score for each round on your phone. If you notice an error, send your recorded scores to the organizer on the same day, after the session.
  7. After each round, the player with the most wins on each court moves UP one court and the player with the fewest moves DOWN (ties: points, then point difference, then a coin toss done by the app). Nobody moves down from the last court in use.
  8. At the end of each session, remaining shuttlecocks on each court are distributed to the top scorer(s) on that court.
  9. New shuttlecocks are ONLY for official ladder matches. Use old/used shuttlecocks while waiting between rounds or during casual play.
  10. The court where you finish each session is your starting court the following week. Court positions carry over week to week.
  11. All court allocations are final and decided by the admin. If a higher-caliber spare player joins, you may be displaced from your court position.
  12. Maintain respectful communication and resolve disputes through the admin only. League polls may be conducted via the Facebook group.
  13. Missing a session without notice will move your court one down at the start of the following session.
  14. Bring your own water bottle and towel. No glass containers in the gymnasium. Wear non-marking indoor shoes at all times.
  15. Each session has two ladder rounds; every court finishes a round before the next begins. After the second round, courts are free for casual games or drills.
  16. If a court has fewer than 4 players (due to absences or odd numbers), it switches to singles round-robin format. 3 players play 3 singles games; 2 players play best-of-3 singles. Nobody plays alone: a single leftover player joins the last court in use as a fifth player. When more than 24 play, the extra players join the bottom courts as a fifth player (Court 6, then 5, then 4): a court of five plays five doubles games to 15 and each player sits out one.
  17. Registration through this app confirms your interest and waiver acceptance. Court placement is handled separately by the admin — registering does not automatically assign you to a court.
  18. The app tracks all scores, rankings, and court movements in real time. Players can view standings, game history, and session results at any time.
