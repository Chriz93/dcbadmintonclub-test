## 2. How the attendance adjustment decides (the rules the tests check)

These rules apply only to a running session, and only to courts with no score yet in the current round. A court with
scores keeps its players until the round ends.

1. **Absent players** leave their court.
2. **A player marked present after being taken off** goes back to the court they left. If that court is full, has scores
   or is unavailable, they are placed like the players in step 3.
3. **Players who must move** are placed one at a time: players on an unavailable court, the most recent arrivals on a
   court over five, and returning players who can't go back. Each goes to, in order of preference:
   - the nearest court in use **below** with room;
   - otherwise, a free court below, if another of these players can join them there (so a group from an unavailable court
     keeps playing together);
   - otherwise, the nearest court in use above with room;
   - last, any free court.
4. **Late players** move down one court, as in the late rule above.
5. **Nobody plays alone.** A lone player joins the next court in use below; on the bottom court in use, they join the
   court above as its fifth player (the existing rule).
6. **Nothing else moves.** Nobody is promoted because someone else is missing, and short courts are not merged: a court
   of two plays best of three, three play three singles games, four play three doubles games to 21, and five play five
   doubles games to 15, each sitting out once.
7. **Refused, and nothing changes:**
   - exactly one player left;
   - more players than the available courts hold (five each);
   - a lone player with nowhere to go;
   - closing a court that already has scores.

   The reason is shown.
8. **Initial allocation stays separate.** Start Session still seats the night by ranking: players who are coming, by
   earned court, four per court, with extras as fifth players on Courts 6, 5, 4 and so on. Adjust courts never re-ranks.

**Assumption to confirm.** Votes known before the session (players who declined in time) are part of that initial,
ranked seating. Absences discovered at the gym use Adjust courts, which preserves assignments. If the organizer wants
declines handled the preserving way too, that is a small change to Start Session.

## 3. How to use it (organizer)

- **Mark absences on a phone.**
  1. Admin → 📋 Attendance.
  2. Tap ✅ Present, ⏰ Late or ❌ Absent next to each player; each tap saves at once.
  3. The bar at the top says how many court changes are waiting.
- **Adjust courts.**
  1. Press **🔁 Adjust courts**. The preview lists every change, for example "Asha is absent — off Court 2." or "Dev was
     the only player left on Court 4, so joins Court 5".
  2. If you want, change a move's court, or open "Courts unavailable tonight".
  3. Press **✓ Apply changes**, or **Cancel** (Escape also closes the preview).
  4. If the only news is a late player who has to stay, Apply records that they stay and says "Recorded for Round N
     — no court changes". They won't be moved later.
- **Undo.** After applying, the "Last court adjustment" card has **↶ Undo this adjustment**. The ↶ pill at the bottom and
  Tools → Undo work too. Each press goes back one step.
- **Late arrival.** Tap ⏰ Late on the Attendance tab (applied with the next Adjust courts), or Session → Late Arrivals →
  Mark Late (applied at once).
- **Waiver records.**
  1. Admin → 🖋 Waivers.
  2. **⬇ Record** next to a player downloads a readable file: every acceptance, each with the exact wording, version,
     fingerprint, UTC and Ottawa time, device time zone, how it was accepted, and the registration reference.
     **⬇ All acceptances (CSV)** downloads everything; **⬇ Wording** downloads a version's exact text.
  3. **Make current** changes the version new sign-ups see; registered players are then asked to accept it on Home.
  4. Players → 📄 Export now downloads the same record.

## 7. Limitations and assumptions

- **Passing tests are not proof that there are no bugs.** They show that the behaviours they name work in the situations
  they create. Exploratory testing found defects that the scripted tests had not (section 5).
- **The browser tests use a stand-in, not Supabase.** They run the real page against an in-memory copy of the TEST API
  (`mock-supabase.ts`). The database rules are tested separately on a local Postgres rehearsal. That rehearsal copies
  Supabase's roles, its default table privileges (added in this release) and its sign-in functions, but not the rest of
  Supabase: sign-in emails, rate limits, the authenticator step, the API gateway or the Supabase dashboard.
- **Accessibility.** Automated checks (axe, WCAG 2.2 A/AA) cover every page, tab and main dialog for an organizer and a
  player, plus keyboard focus, reduced motion and 44px touch targets. Nobody has tested with a screen reader or a
  switch device. Contrast is checked only in the states the tests display.
- **Late arrivals.** The organizer decides who counts as more than 5 minutes late; the app applies the move.
- **Initial allocation** is unchanged (section 2, assumption to confirm).
- **Waiver.**
  - v2 is a draft for a lawyer to review. It is not legal advice, and nothing in it claims that players give up every
    claim (docs/30).
  - Records start with this release. Earlier registrations get no invented acceptance; the players are asked to accept
    the current version the next time they open Home.
  - Acceptance records can't be changed, so a restore from backup can only add them to an empty table.
- **Backups.** Until the pending automation patch is approved, the daily backup does not include `waiver_versions`,
  `waiver_acceptances` or `environment`. On production, apply it in the same release as L20 (section 9).
- **Fonts.** Barlow and Barlow Condensed load from Google Fonts. Offline, or if they are blocked, the page falls back to
  system fonts. Google receives the visitor's IP address when it serves the fonts.
- **Reference sites.** smashlytics.net and griffjonesgolf.com were used as inspiration only; no text, images or code
  was copied.
